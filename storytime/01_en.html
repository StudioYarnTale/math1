The Tale of Princess 👑 Zero, Prince One
and the White Rose of Love 🤍🌹

That evening the little dog 🐶Pippa lay at the feet of Vasily Voldemarovich, softly whimpering. Usually she was cheerful and lively, always ready to play ball or run around the yard. But today she looked confused.

Pippa stared at the long line with a point that Anastasia had drawn on a sheet of her notebook.
— Woof… woof… I am completely confused… — Pippa said quietly, lowering her ears. — I do not understand anything. Perhaps I am a silly little dog… — These numbers… they keep going somewhere… and I do not understand where they come from…

Nearby sat 🦊Liumy, the little fox cub.

At first he ran around the sheet, as he always did, asking his endless questions one after another:
— What if the number goes this way? — What if it turns? — And why does zero have nothing?

But then, as often happened with him, he suddenly grew quiet. He sat down. Furrowed his little brow. And began to think.

He was confused too, to be honest. He both understood and did not understand. A pebble, a coin, or a ball — those were clear, you could touch them. But numbers… how could you touch them? And if you cannot touch them — does that mean they are not really there? Then why were they invented?

Anastasia👧 looked at both of them and smiled.

She stroked Pippa gently on the head and said:
— Do not be afraid, Pippa. Numbers are not frightening symbols. They are the inhabitants of a very beautiful kingdom. Now I will tell you a story.

She knew: Pippa needed comfort, and Liumy needed a mystery.
— I will tell you a story, — she said softly. — A story about Princess Zero and Prince One.

And Pippa immediately lifted her head. She loved stories.

Liumy moved closer too — his eyes were already shining with curiosity. He loved stories just as much.

🌟 Princess Zero 👸
— Long, long ago, — Anastasia began, — when there were no numbers, no counting, and no problems, there lived only one Princess. Her name was Zero.

She lived on a very long road called the Number Line.

This road stretched far in both directions — but back then no one lived there. Only she alone.

Princess Zero was quiet, wise, and very calm. Sometimes it seemed that she was completely empty — as if she were nothing, as if she did not exist at all. But in truth, she was the beginning of everything. Inside her were hidden all the numbers that were yet to come.

She was the source. She was silence. She was waiting.

⚡ The Arrival of Prince One 🤴
— One day, — Anastasia continued, — a traveller appeared on the road. Straight. Brave. Proud and full of energy. — It was Prince One!

He walked forward confidently and suddenly saw Princess Zero.

He stopped. Because he had never before seen such calm and quiet beauty, and such a wise and deep gaze in which infinity ∞ was reflected. He stepped toward her, bent his knee, and offered her a white rose as a symbol of his love and admiration.
— I want to stand beside you, — he said. And confidently he took his place to the right of her on the number road.

Like this:

Zero  One
  0   1

At that moment the silence came alive. And movement began.

📏 The First Measure

Pippa was now listening very carefully.
— And then what happened? — she asked quietly.

Anastasia picked up a pencil.
— Then a measure appeared, — she said. — It was necessary to understand how far Prince One stood from Princess Zero.
— The distance between them would be called One. That would be the very first step in our world.

Anastasia placed a small forest nut 🌰 next to Prince One.

The distance from Zero to One became the first measure in this world.

Anastasia drew a small mark on the line.

🌰 The Birth of Natural Numbers
— But the world cannot stop, — she continued.
— Anastasia decided to keep moving forward.

She took small forest nuts — the very same ones they had gathered together in autumn under the hazel bushes, when the leaves had already turned yellow and rustled softly beneath their feet.

The nuts softly knocked against each other in a small woven basket 🧺.

She began to mark equal steps.

One nut 🌰 — one step. She measured another distance of the same length and placed a new point. The number 2 appeared. To be sure, she placed two nuts beside it 🌰🌰.

Then another step. And another nut. The number 3 appeared 🌰🌰🌰.

And so it went on. Step by step. Point by point.

And along the number road new inhabitants appeared:

2🌰🌰, 3🌰🌰🌰, 4🌰🌰🌰🌰, 5🌰🌰🌰🌰🌰…

They stood at exactly equal distances from each other. Because each step was equal to one unit.
— Now you try it yourself, — Anastasia said, handing Pippa the little basket of nuts.
— I understand! — cried Liumy. — I want to try too! Let me place a nut as well!

Pippa and Liumy began drawing numbers along the line and labeling them, happily placing little piles of nuts beside each number — until the basket was empty.
— What comes next? We want to keep playing this game! — cried Liumy.
— Then continue, — answered Anastasia. — Just imagine that you are placing nuts, even if you have none left. It is that simple!

🌱 The World Grows

Pippa no longer looked sad. She watched closely as Liumy drew new points and labeled numbers.
— So… numbers are steps? — she asked.

Anastasia smiled.
— Yes, Pippa. Numbers are steps along the road.

Each new step is a new point. Each nut is a new measure. And the entire world of numbers grew from one simple action: adding one step forward.

🌸 Comfort for Pippa

Pippa lifted her reddish ears.
— So… I am not confused? — she asked quietly.
— No, — Anastasia answered. — You are simply standing at the very beginning of a long road.

And that is a very good place to be. Because right here:

👸 lives Princess Zero
👑 stands Prince One
🌹 blooms the white rose
📏 and the journey begins

And from here all mathematics begins.

🌙 A Small Conclusion

Pippa smiled — calmly now.
— I am no longer afraid of numbers, — she said. — They are simply inhabitants of the road…

Liumy jumped with delight:
— Look! I have already reached the end of the room! May I continue outside, beyond the window?
— Of course you may, — Anastasia answered and added quietly:
— Each of us can walk along this road. Step by step. The most important thing is not to hurry. And to remember: every big number once began with its very first step.
— And where does this road end? — asked Pippa.
— And what if I walk in the opposite direction? — chattered mischievous Liumy. He always wanted to do something the other way around.

Anastasia smiled.
— That is something I will tell you about a little later…
