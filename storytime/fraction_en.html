🏰 Kingdom of Numbers

Story 4.
The Mysterious Point and the Great Fraction Debate

At that moment 🐶Pippa was sitting quietly nearby, drawing something in her own doggy way. She took a pencil and — tap! — placed a tiny dot exactly between 0 and 1 on the number line. 🦊Liumy the Fox Cub noticed it at once — his little ears twitched:

— No-no-no! — he cried. — That is wrong! There is no number there — it is empty!

🐶Pippa stubbornly tilted her head and crossed her paws: — But what if there is?

The children and animals began arguing, and then Vasily Voldemarovich joined the conversation. He looked at everyone over his glasses and calmly said: — Anastasia… on the kitchen table Auntie Polya has brought us a 🍰pie.

Anastasia ran off at once and returned a minute later carrying a large fragrant 🍰pie. Everyone grew quiet, even 🦊Liumy stopped wagging his tail. Vasily Voldemarovich took a knife. — Let us see… how many of us are here?

🦊Liumy shouted first: — Four! — Correct, — nodded Vasily Voldemarovich. — Then we shall divide the 🍰pie into four parts. He carefully cut the 🍰pie. One… two… three… Four identical golden slices lay on the plate. — One piece for each of you, — he said.

Everyone happily took their pieces. — Now tell me… — continued Grandfather. — What is one piece called? Anastasia wanted to answer but stopped, giving her friends a chance. 🦊Liumy thought, scratched behind his ear, and suddenly cried: — We divided one 🍰pie into four parts! That means one piece is one quarter!

— Excellent, — smiled Vasily Voldemarovich and wrote: 1/4. — And if we take two pieces? — he asked. 🦊Liumy counted quickly: — Then that is 2/4! — And three? — 3/4!

Everyone smiled, but 🐶Pippa looked again at her point between 0 and 1. Vasily Voldemarovich noticed this, stepped toward the line, and said: — This point lies exactly in the middle.

🦊Liumy suddenly slapped his knee: — I understand! It is because the point is in the middle! It is like we divided the segment into two parts! That means it is one half! Vasily Voldemarovich wrote: 1/2.

But Anastasia narrowed her eyes. She never agreed immediately — she always wanted to understand things fully. — Not quite, — she said calmly. — Look carefully.

Anastasia took a pencil and carefully divided the same segment into four equal parts. She marked the points. One… two… three… Then she pointed to the very same middle point: — Look! If we divide into four parts, this point is the second of four. That means it is 2/4!

🦊Liumy even jumped up, his red tail swishing angrily: — No! I said it first! It is 1/2! You mixed everything up! But Anastasia stubbornly shook her head: — Even if you said it first, I am still right. It is 2/4!

🐶Pippa sat silently, looking from 1/2 to 2/4. Then she barked softly: — I do not understand anything again… We must figure this out! She jumped up and brought a rope and scissors. — Now I will make peace between you!

🐶Pippa laid the rope on the table: — This is our segment. Then she began cutting: Snip! Snip! Snip! She divided it into ten equal pieces. 🐶Pippa took five pieces, put them together, and placed them at the middle of the long rope.

— Look! — she said proudly. — See! This is five out of ten — 5/10. And this is also the middle! No more arguing! 🦊Liumy frowned, Anastasia leaned closer. They looked… and looked… And suddenly 🦊Liumy said slowly: — Wait… Do you mean this is the same place?

Anastasia suddenly clapped her hands: — Exactly! It is the same point!

🐶Pippa wagged her tail happily: — See! I made peace between you! At that moment Vasily Voldemarovich stepped in. He adjusted his glasses and wrote: 1/2 = 2/4 = 5/10.

— Children… you have just discovered a great law of the Kingdom of Numbers. If different fractions show the same place on the line, then they are equal. 🦊Liumy opened his mouth in surprise: — So… different numbers can mean the same thing?

— Yes, — smiled Vasily Voldemarovich. — They may be written differently, but they show the same part. It is like dividing a 🍰pie in different ways — half always remains half.

🐶Pippa smiled softly: — So… fractions can change their clothes? Vasily Voldemarovich nodded: — Exactly. They can change their clothes but remain the same number. 🦊Liumy jumped up: — That means between 0 and 1 there is not just one number — there is a whole family of fractions!

Anastasia looked at the long line and whispered: — It seems our Kingdom has become even bigger… And the number line lay before them — full of new inhabitants waiting to be discovered… 🌟
